# TEAM_Code_Blooded_Heart_Health_Checker

Problem Statement:
Anemia is one of India’s most widespread health problems: NFHS-5 found 67.1% of children and 59.1% of adolescent girls anemic. Surveys have found out that around 50% of Indias population is anemic.
Yet checking hemoglobin or general blood diagnostics still depends on blood samples, trained phlebotomists, labs and waiting time, and diagnostic care built on blood draws has not reached the last mile. 
In remote villages, schools and small clinics it is difficult to use traditional diagnostic methods by taking blood samples due to lack of facilities, fear of sample contamination, stigma against drawing blood, lack of sterilisation etc which can cause diseases.

Solution:
India needs a needle free way to screen and monitor heart health so we can bridge the gap in healthcare and reach the last mile, That is where our HEART HEALTH CHECKER comes in.
Our device consists of a max30102 sensor that provides screening based on ppg technology by calculating the interference between hemoglobin and red and infrared waveforms to provide accurate data to user.
The device provides readings of SpO2, HRV(heart rate variability), bpm, waveform and hemoglobin to user instantly, eliminating the need for costly lab kits and providing a quick, easy and painless diagnostic method.
